Getting supporting statements

Other people can support your candidacy by submitting their own nomination for you. Share this link with them:

{{ request.scheme }}://{{ request.get_host }}{{ nomination_create_url }}

  • They need to sign in with their own python.org account.
  • Ask them to leave “self nomination” unchecked and enter your name and {{ request.user.email }}, the email address on your python.org account, so their nomination can be matched to you.
  • Once a supporting nomination is linked to you, accept it from your nominations. Only accepted nominations are published.
  • Supporting nominations must be submitted before nominations close.
